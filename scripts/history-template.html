A year of sky, water, and suns

Between March 2025 and June 2026, SkyBloom was built, broken, rebuilt, renamed, and finally released. Every screenshot below is anchored to a real commit day — proof of the work, the pivots, and the quiet breakthroughs.

22 March 2025

Genesis

“A block, some water, and a dream.”

Two near-simultaneous commits create magical-carpet. The world is flat, the carpet is a blue block, and the water is a pale plane. The only goal: prove that flying feels good.

  • 1526696e — Initial commit
  • 0dda3ff2 — V2
  • e0886f6d — V2.1
23–31 March 2025

The Mobile Crusade

“Make it run everywhere, make it pretty, make it not crash.”

Over one hundred commits in a single month. The creator battles mobile FPS, water that flashes brown, terrain that glitches, and a sky that somehow ends up with three competing suns.

  • 23 Mar — Optmization ended 60FPS everywhere
  • 29 Mar — Remove water system and references
  • 30 Mar — Refactoring Atmosphere systems - cloud disabled
  • 31 Mar — SUN VISIBILITY FIXED / side: THREE.FrontSide + 3
  • 31 Mar — Consolidate multiple sun implementations
  • 31 Mar — MULTIPLAYER V1
April 2025

Refactoring Fever

“Tear it down and rebuild it cleanly.”

The prototype is re-architected with an ECS, SystemManager, state management, and dedicated renderer/quality managers. It is an attempt to turn a wild garden into a cathedral.

  • ec76c05 — REFACTORING PLAN
  • bcd0ba2 — Implement ECS architecture with SystemManager
  • 78dc417 — Merge slow-mode branch into main
May – August 2025

The Quiet Season

“Rest and planning.”

The repository goes almost silent. No screenshots, no commits. The project waits, like a seed under winter soil.

“No commits. No screenshots. Just quiet.”

September – November 2025

The Cozy World Overhaul

“It is not an engine; it is a place.”

The game returns with a new goal: feel small, gentle, and alive. Water becomes simpler and more reflective. Real tree models arrive. Day, night, stars, and moon settle into place.

  • 7 Nov — Transform world into cozy, living landscape
  • 7 Nov — Replace with ultra-simple flat water - Magic Carpet 1994 style
  • 7 Nov — Restore working water from main branch
  • 8 Nov — Add 6 real tree models from Kenney Nature Kit
  • 8 Nov — Replace simple water with Three.js Water for realistic reflections
  • 8 Nov — Fix jagged shoreline and terrain bleeding through water
June 2026

The Agentic Arena

“From game to benchmark.”

The project is renamed SkyBloom. A polished title screen appears. Seeded 12-gate races, local ghosts, the window.agentAPI, and a reference SimpleBot turn the world into a public stage for human-vs-agent flight.

  • 6e85cec — rename project references to skybloom
  • e69fbc1 — Agent API + fair human-vs-agent racing
  • 90a1692 — Twilight Glass design system + new title screen
  • 18ff9f3 — prepare SkyBloom race and agent release loop
  • 99d6578 — add pages deploy and friendly play setup
  • 19b2dfb — add procedural Ennio Morricone-style music system
